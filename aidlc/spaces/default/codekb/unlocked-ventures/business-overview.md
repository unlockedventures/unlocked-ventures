# Business Overview

## Domain and Purpose
Unlocked Ventures combines a public marketing site with a Payload editorial portal. This focused baseline covers episode creation and its AI tools; it is observed implementation, not approved requirements.

## Key Functionality
Editors select a channel and thumbnail, save a draft, transcribe linked videos, generate editable podcast content, and optionally generate audio. Draft/Complete status controls public visibility according to the collection guidance. Featured controls homepage placement. See component-inventory.md for the inspected surface and api-documentation.md for generation boundaries.
